Early Warning Indicators Your Greensboro AC or Heat Pump Requires Service

Catching mechanical failure indicators early prevents total system shutdown during July heatwaves. Monitoring cycle durations, electrical draw on dual run capacitors, and temperature splits across the evaporator coil ensures minor component wear is resolved before compressor damage occurs.

  • Supply registers delivering lukewarm air while the outdoor heat pump or condenser is actively cycling
  • Rapid short-cycling or continuous fan operation failing to satisfy the digital thermostat setpoint
  • Ice formation or heavy frost across the copper suction line and indoor aluminum evaporator coil
  • Unusual grinding, rattling, or electrical humming sounds from the condenser fan motor or scroll compressor
