Frequently asked questions

How does cold-climate heat pump technology work in Greensboro winters?

Cold-climate inverter heat pumps utilize variable-speed scroll compressors and enhanced vapor injection to compress low-density outdoor refrigerant vapor efficiently even when outside temperatures drop into the single digits, delivering comfortable 105°F supply air without engaging auxiliary electric heat strips.

What causes a heat pump to freeze into a solid block of ice?

A solid ice buildup on an outdoor heat pump indicates a malfunction in the defrost control board, a defective ambient air temperature sensor, a faulty reversing valve, or low refrigerant charge preventing the coil from warming during defrost cycles.
