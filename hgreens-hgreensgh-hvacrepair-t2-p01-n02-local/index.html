Borescopic Heat Exchanger Crack Testing & Carbon Monoxide Prevention

Thermal expansion and contraction over years of seasonal cycling can cause microscopic hairline stress fractures in stamped steel or tubular furnace heat exchangers. If cracks develop, toxic combustion gases—including colorless, odorless carbon monoxide—can be drawn directly into your indoor supply airstream by the blower motor. We inspect every cell using high-definition fiber-optic borescopes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my furnace heat exchanger is cracked?

Warning signs include soot buildup around the furnace burner compartment, a yellow or flickering burner flame instead of a crisp blue cone, strange chemical odors, water pooling around the furnace base, or household carbon monoxide alarms sounding.

What is the life expectancy of a natural gas furnace in Greensboro?

With regular annual autumn maintenance, a quality residential gas furnace typically lasts 15 to 20 years. Units operating beyond 15 years should undergo thorough annual borescopic heat exchanger testing to guarantee continued safety.
