Why Dedicated Dehumidification Outperforms Standard AC Moisture Removal

Central air conditioners only dehumidify when there is a cooling demand. On cloudy, rainy 75°F summer afternoons in Greensboro, your thermostat may not call for cooling, allowing humidity inside the home to spike unchecked. A whole-home dehumidifier monitors relative humidity independently, drawing moist air through dedicated return ductwork and returning dry, fresh air throughout the home.

Frequently asked questions

What is the ideal indoor humidity level for homes in Greensboro NC?

Indoor relative humidity should be maintained between 45% and 50%. Levels above 55% foster dust mite propagation and mold growth, while levels below 35% cause dry skin, sinus irritation, and hardwood floor cracking.

Does a whole-home dehumidifier connect to my existing air ductwork?

Yes. A whole-home dehumidifier is typically installed in the crawl space, basement, or attic alongside the primary air handler, tying into the return and supply plenums to distribute dehumidified air evenly.
